1 · The three "Server Components render" errors solved & fixed

"Unable to change chart type… add image to library… add view."

  • The cause wasn't the chart code. We rebuilt the exact production setup and all three buttons worked — then reproduced your exact error by one means only: an expired/invalid login session. When that happens PRISM refused the action (correct) but showed it as a masked server crash (very much not correct).
  • Fixed: an expired session now takes you to the login page with a return link, on every save-style action — no more fake crashes. (This only ever showed in production; in development the login check is off, which is why it never appeared in testing.)
  • One check closes your specific incident: if reloading PRISM around then bounced you to the login page, this was exactly it. If you were still logged in, say so — there's a recorded error trail we can pull.

Search covers every dataset in PRISM (same search as /datasets). Not-ready matches show why + the one-click fix, instead of disappearing.

The born-interactive trap you spotted is real — and deeper. Charts created interactive from the start (like this import's) have no route back into the picker, ever — and under the hood no command to rebind one even exists yet. It's being built. One deliberate caveat: a chart's data connection is shared with published pages, so changing (or unbinding) the dataset of an already-published chart routes through review instead of applying silently.

Also fixed from the thread: the dataset page's "Bound consumers" list can't see charts at all today (only tables/displays) — that's why it claimed nothing used your dataset. It will list the charts, with links to their articles.

4 · Chart looks: labels, colours, the source line three bugs

"'over 78 5%' reads as one… labels on top of each other… Occitanie is a different colour from Nouvelle-Aquitaine even though both are 26… the chart shouldn't have that source footnote."

  • Labels: label + value are joined by a bare space — the hover tooltip already says "Over 78: 5%"; the canvas catches up. Plus a spread-apart pass so labels can't collide.
  • Colours: only the first bar after sorting gets the accent, so a 26-vs-26 tie colours two ways. Fix: equal values share the accent. Emphasis controls (highlight a bar, good/bad tones, rank shading) already exist in the engine — they get buttons. Free colour-picking stays out: charts keep the site palette.
  • Source footnote: with no hand-typed source, charts auto-print our internal dataset ref as "Source: …" — and clearing the field doesn't stop it (that's the empty-Spec-box mystery). Internal refs stop printing entirely.

5 · The X/Y axis controls you were right — they moved

"Should have the ability to add X and Y axis… (this seemed to be there?)"

They did exist. The old chart engine's axis pickers were retired on 4 July — five days before your review — and the "which column is X, which is Y" choice moved into the bind-data flow, with nothing left behind pointing at the new home. A pointer is being added ("Axes come from the data binding — Change dataset…"). Written axis titles never existed in either engine; if a real chart needs them, that's a separate product call, not a bug.

7 · Called out, deliberately not changing

  • Free colour pickers — emphasis controls yes; arbitrary hex no. A site's charts stay one family.
  • Binding from the dataset page — it gains visibility (which charts/articles use it); binding stays on the article side, where the context is.
  • Authored axis titles — see §5; a separate product call if a real chart needs them.
Under the hood? Healthier than expected — one render path, one bind machinery, very little dead code; the §1 crashes are the exception, not the pattern. Full engineering review + slice plan: prism-docs · plans/charts-tables-binding-ux/2026-07-09-editor-round-2.md.
