Charts — Data tab, bind-to-existing, display fixes

Editor-review round 2026-07-09 (France camping statistics). Three families of fix: the chart Data tab learns what every other bound section already shows (dataset name, link, change, unbind); the bind picker stops dead-ending when datasets exist but aren't "ready"; and three chart-render defects (pie label glue, tie-color asymmetry, internal source footnote). E2

Bind picker: existing datasets stay findable — even when not "ready"

The picker only lists datasets with a completed revision, from the 40 newest. A dataset that exists as a draft — or is older than the cap — vanishes, and the modal's only affordance becomes "+ New dataset ↗". That's the trap the editor hit: the France dataset existed, the picker couldn't say so.

E3

Chart display: label glue, tie colors, the phantom source line

Three render defects from the review. Pie labels concatenate label + value with a bare space ("over 78 5%") and can overlap on small adjacent slices; bar charts give two equal values different colors (only the first max gets the accent); and charts bound to internal datasets auto-print a "Source: … · r6" footnote that isn't a real source.

Now
French campers by age group
Share of campers, 2024
Source: France camping statistics · cc.camping-statistics-multiple-locations.france · r6

"over 78 5%" reads as one number-soup; two 5% slices stack their labels; and the footnote leaks an internal dataset key onto the published page.

New
French campers by age group
Share of campers, 2024
Source: internal dataset ref

Separator + muted value (the tooltip already does "Over 78: 5%" — the canvas catches up); labels get a push-apart pass per side (deterministic, no overlaps); the auto source line is suppressed for internal datasets — only a real external source (or hand-typed attribution) prints.

Equal values, equal color — every bar tied at the max shares the accent. The existing (unsurfaced) semantic controls — highlight a named category, good/bad tones, rank gradient — get inspector controls, so editors steer emphasis without a raw color picker.

E4

Dataset page: consumers that include charts, and link back

The dataset page's "Bound consumers" list is computed from section rows — chart bindings live on the chart entity, so a dataset used only by charts claims "No sections bind to this dataset yet." The fix makes chart consumers visible and clickable; a bind action from this side stays out of scope for now (the editor-side flows are the campaign path).
